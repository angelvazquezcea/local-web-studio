# Professional services: accountants, gestorías, lawyers, architects, consultants, insurance

## Goals
Trust and qualified enquiries; explain services clearly; show the people; local authority.

## Audiences
Self-employed, small businesses, farmers (grants, CAP/PAC paperwork), families (inheritance,
property), companies.

## Must-have sections
Hero: who you help + where + Call · services grouped by client type (business / individuals /
agriculture) · how we work (first meeting, fees approach) · team with qualifications ·
**professional registration data** · resources/FAQ (deadlines, documents to bring) · location &
hours · contact/appointment.

## Hard rules
- Regulated professions must show professional association and registration number; bar and
  college advertising rules apply (no comparative or sensationalist claims; no guaranteed
  outcomes).
- Confidentiality: contact forms shouldn't invite detailed case information; privacy notice
  covers enquiries.

## Schema
`AccountingService`, `LegalService`/`Attorney`, `ProfessionalService`, `InsuranceAgency`,
`RealEstateAgent`, with `areaServed`, `knowsLanguage`.

## CTAs
Call, book a first meeting, WhatsApp for quick questions (if they accept it).

## Photos
Team portraits (real, warm), office, the town — avoid stock handshakes and gavels.

## FAQ ideas
What documents do I need for X? Fees? Do you handle agricultural grants? Online meetings?
Deadlines this quarter?

## Discovery questions
Ideal client types? Services to push/avoid? Fee policy to publish? Association rules?
Languages? Seasonal peaks (tax campaign, grant windows)?

## Pitfalls
Jargon; generic stock; outdated deadlines.

## Upsells
Seasonal deadline banners in the care plan; resource pages; partnership (they refer clients
needing websites — see `partnerships`).
