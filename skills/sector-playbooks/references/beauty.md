# Hair, beauty, barbers, aesthetics

## Goals
Bookings, filling quiet days, showcasing style, retaining staff/hiring.

## Audiences
Local clients by age/style, brides/events, men (barbers), tourists (less).

## Must-have sections
Hero with work + Book · services with prices/durations (if public) · the team (stylists and
specialties) · gallery of real work · products/brands used · booking info & policies (late
arrivals, cancellations) · hours · reviews · contact.

## Hard rules
- Aesthetic/medical treatments (botox, fillers, lasers) are regulated — health-practitioner
  rules and advertising limits apply; no before/after promises of results where restricted.
- Photos of clients only with consent.

## Schema
`HairSalon`, `BeautySalon`, `NailSalon`, `DaySpa`, `BarberShop`-style via `HairSalon`.

## CTAs
Book (their booking app), WhatsApp, Call.

## Photos
Real work (consented), the space, the team, products. Consistent light; vertical crops for
mobile.

## FAQ ideas
Do I need to book? How long does X take? Cancellation policy? Products used? Bridal packages?

## Discovery questions
Booking app used? Signature services? Price list public? Staff who want to be featured? Which
days are quiet? Instagram as the gallery source?

## Pitfalls
Embedding a heavy Instagram widget; outdated price lists; using clients' photos without consent.

## Upsells
Gallery updates in the care plan; gift voucher page; hiring page.
