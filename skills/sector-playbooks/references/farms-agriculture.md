# Farms, livestock, crops, cooperatives

## Goals
Many farms don't sell to the public (integrated pig/poultry farms, dairy selling to a
cooperative). Their site is about **image and trust**: for the integrator/buyer, for
neighbours and the town, for recruiting workers, for suppliers, for grants and certifications,
and for family pride. Farms that sell direct (meat boxes, eggs, vegetables, honey) need
ordering and pickup/delivery information.

## Audiences
Buyers/integrators, potential workers, suppliers and vets, neighbours, public bodies, direct
customers (if any), schools (only if visits are allowed).

## Must-have sections
Hero with drone/landscape image · who we are (family, generations, since) · what we produce
and how (method, scale in facts: heads, hectares, production) · animal welfare and
biosecurity (if livestock) · certifications · environment (manure management, energy,
water — only true facts) · the team / work with us · gallery · contact & location (or service
area) · FAQ.

## Hard rules
- **Biosecurity**: many livestock farms forbid visits. Then: no visit offers, no "come and see
  us", no visitor form; you may say visits are not possible for animal health reasons. The
  address may be hidden on Google (service area) — keep site and profile consistent.
- Animal welfare wording: factual, no claims you can't support; avoid imagery that could be
  misread (dead animals, injured animals, overcrowding, staff without protective gear).
- Certifications (organic, welfare labels, PDO/PGI): only with the certificate in hand; use the
  official logo rules.
- Direct sales of food: hygiene registration numbers and labelling rules apply.

## Schema
`LocalBusiness` (no Farm type) with `description`, `geo`, `areaServed`; `Store` if they sell
direct; `foundingDate` if known.

## CTAs
Call/WhatsApp the owner; email for companies; "Work with us" (job application); for direct sales
"Order on WhatsApp".

## Photos
Drone wide shots (season with green fields), facilities exterior (clean, tidy), the family/team
at work with protective gear, machinery, crops/animals in good conditions, details (feed,
water systems), products. Avoid interiors if biosecurity-sensitive.

## FAQ ideas
Can I visit? (if not, why) · Do you sell directly? · Where do your products go? · Are you
hiring? · How do you manage manure/smells? (neighbour concern, only if the client wants it).

## Discovery questions
Who buys your production? What do you want them to think? Generations and dates? Scale
figures you're happy to publish? Certifications? Hiring needs? Visits allowed? Neighbours'
concerns? Grant/sustainability projects? Drone footage available or wanted?

## Pitfalls
Offering visits/tours when not allowed; inventing sustainability claims; stock images of
farms from other climates; showing sensitive interiors; too many languages without a reason
(add them when buyers or workers need them).

## Upsells
Drone video (partner); recruitment page; multilingual for foreign buyers/workers; printed
QR signage at the gate; Google profile with correct pin for lorries and suppliers.
