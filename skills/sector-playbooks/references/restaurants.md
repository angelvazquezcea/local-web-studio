# Restaurants, bars, cafés

## Goals
Reservations and walk-ins, fewer "are you open?" calls, events and groups, takeaway orders,
appearing in "restaurant near me".

## Audiences
Locals, tourists (often foreign, on phones, nearby right now), groups/events, pilgrims/hikers.

## Must-have sections
Hero with signature dish/space + Book/Call · **hours (today highlighted)** · menu as HTML text
(not only PDF/image) with prices, allergens info · specials/daily menu (easy to update) ·
the place (terrace, interior, capacity) · groups & events · story/chef · reviews · how to get
here & parking · contact.

## Hard rules
- **Allergen information** available (EU: the 14 allergens; at least "ask staff" note plus
  info on request).
- Prices with VAT; supplements (terrace, bread) stated if charged.
- Alcohol promotion rules where applicable.

## Schema
`Restaurant`, `CafeOrCoffeeShop`, `BarOrPub` with `servesCuisine`, `menu` URL,
`acceptsReservations`, `openingHoursSpecification`, `priceRange`.

## CTAs
Book (their reservation tool) / Call / WhatsApp for groups / Directions. Takeaway: order by
phone/WhatsApp or their delivery partner.

## Photos
Dishes (natural light, real portions), the room at service, terrace, the team/kitchen, exterior
for recognition.

## FAQ ideas
Do you take bookings? Gluten-free/vegetarian options? Terrace? Pets? Children's menu? Groups?
Parking? Card payment? Opening on holidays?

## Discovery questions
Who updates the menu and how often? Daily menu? Reservation tool? Busiest/emptiest days?
Events? Languages of tourists? Signature dishes and producers they buy from?

## Pitfalls
Menu only as a heavy PDF or Instagram photo; wrong holiday hours; autoplay music/video.

## Upsells
Menu-update service in the care plan; multilingual menu; QR menu at tables; Google profile
menu and photo updates.
