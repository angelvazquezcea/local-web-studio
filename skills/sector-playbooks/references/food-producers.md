# Food producers: bakeries, cheese, wine, honey, preserves, meat

## Goals
Sell more direct (shop, markets, orders), get stockists and restaurants, tell the origin story,
appear for "<product> <town/region>".

## Audiences
Local customers, tourists, shops/restaurants (B2B), gift buyers, export buyers.

## Must-have sections
Hero with product and origin · products (photo, short description, format/sizes, price if
public) · how it's made (process, ingredients, origin) · where to buy (own shop with hours,
markets calendar, stockists list, delivery/shipping) · for professionals (wholesale contact) ·
story · awards/PDO/PGI (if real) · contact.

## Hard rules
- **No health or nutrition claims** beyond those authorised (EU Reg. 1924/2006). "Healthy",
  "cures", "boosts immunity" → no.
- PDO/PGI and organic logos only if certified; follow the logo rules.
- **Alcohol**: age notice where required; no messages linking alcohol to success, health or
  driving; some countries restrict online sales.
- Online food sales: allergen information before purchase (EU Reg. 1169/2011), sanitary
  registration numbers, shipping cold-chain conditions.

## Schema
`Bakery`, `Winery`, `Store`/`GroceryStore`, `LocalBusiness`; `Product` items only when
there is a real product page with price.

## CTAs
Order on WhatsApp/phone, visit the shop (hours + map), find a stockist, wholesale enquiry.

## Photos
Product close-ups on real surfaces, process (hands, ovens, cellar, hives), place, people,
packaging, markets.

## FAQ ideas
Do you ship? Where can I buy it? Allergens/gluten-free? Shelf life? Custom orders/events?
Visits/tastings (if offered)?

## Discovery questions
Best-selling products? What do they want to sell more of? B2B vs retail share? Do they ship
and how? Certifications and awards (with dates)? Seasonal products? Who updates prices?

## Pitfalls
A shop/cart without logistics behind it; outdated price lists; health claims; missing
allergens.

## Upsells
Order-by-WhatsApp catalogue; product photography (partner); seasonal campaign updates;
stockist map.
