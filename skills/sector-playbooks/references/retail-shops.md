# Shops and local retail

## Goals
Footfall, phone/WhatsApp orders, click-and-collect, being found for "<product> <town>",
showing range and personal service versus big chains.

## Audiences
Locals, nearby towns, gift buyers, tourists.

## Must-have sections
Hero with the shop + Directions/Call · what you sell (categories with photos; brands) ·
services (repairs, alterations, delivery, advice) · offers/news (easy to update) · about the
shop/owner · hours (today highlighted) · location/parking · contact.

## Hard rules
- Prices shown must include VAT for consumers; promotions with dates and conditions.
- Online sales → consumer law (withdrawal right, terms, shipping) — separate e-commerce scope.

## Schema
Most specific `Store` subtype: `HardwareStore`, `ClothingStore`, `ShoeStore`, `PetStore`,
`FurnitureStore`, `BookStore`, `GardenStore`, `Florist`, `JewelryStore`, `SportingGoodsStore`…

## CTAs
Directions, Call, WhatsApp ("do you have…?"), reserve for pickup.

## Photos
Storefront (recognition), interior, products in context, owner/staff, window displays.

## FAQ ideas
Do you have X in stock? Can I reserve? Delivery? Repairs? Gift wrap/vouchers? Parking?

## Discovery questions
Top categories and brands? Do they want online sales or just reservations? Who updates
offers? Seasonal peaks? Loyalty programme?

## Pitfalls
Building a full shop the owner can't maintain; stock that's never current.

## Upsells
WhatsApp catalogue; gift voucher page; seasonal campaigns; simple e-commerce later via a
hosted platform.
