# Agricultural machinery, dealers, rural contractors

## Goals
Get calls for sales, repairs, spare parts and contracting work (silage, harvesting, earthwork);
show stock of new/used machines; build trust in the workshop's speed in the season.

## Audiences
Farmers and cooperatives (mostly on phones, in the field), contractors, public works, buyers
of used machinery from other regions/countries.

## Must-have sections
Hero with machinery at work · services (sales, workshop, parts, contracting, rental) · brands
represented (only official dealerships may use brand logos as dealers — check the agreement) ·
used machinery list (simple, updatable) · workshop & emergency service in season · coverage
area map · team · contact with separate numbers per department.

## Hard rules
- Brand trademarks: use logos only if authorised dealer; otherwise name brands in text
  ("we repair John Deere, Fendt…" is fine as factual service description, avoid implying
  official status).
- Used machinery prices: VAT included or not, stated clearly; "price on request" allowed.

## Schema
`AutoDealer` and `AutoRepair` are meant for cars, so they fit tractors poorly. Use `Store` for
dealers and `LocalBusiness` for workshops and contractors, list services with `makesOffer`,
and set `areaServed`.

## CTAs
Call workshop (primary in season), WhatsApp with photo of the breakdown, call sales, request
contracting quote.

## Photos
Machines at work in local fields, workshop clean and equipped, team, parts counter, delivery
truck, before/after repairs. Real stock photos of each used machine (several angles, hours
meter).

## FAQ ideas
Do you come to the field? Emergency service in harvest? Which brands do you repair? Do you
buy used machines / accept trade-ins? Financing? Shipping of used machines?

## Discovery questions
Which service brings most money? Seasonality? Coverage radius? Who updates the used stock
and how often? Departments and phones? Dealership agreements?

## Pitfalls
Stale used-machinery list (offer a simple editable list or link to their listings on
specialised portals); one phone for everything; heavy catalog PDFs.

## Upsells
Simple used-stock page the client edits (spreadsheet → page) or a care-plan update service;
drone/action video; multilingual for export buyers.
