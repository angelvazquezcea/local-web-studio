# Trades: plumbers, electricians, builders, carpenters, roofers, painters

## Goals
Calls and quote requests for the jobs they want (not all jobs), in the area they want; trust
(licensed, insured, reviews, real work photos); emergencies if they offer them.

## Audiences
Homeowners (often urgent), landlords, communities of owners, small businesses, architects.

## Must-have sections
Hero: trade + area + Call · services list (clear, each with a line) · emergencies (if offered,
with hours) · areas served (towns list) · recent work gallery (before/after) · why us
(licensed, insured, years, guarantees — real) · process/quote · reviews · contact with
"send photos on WhatsApp".

## Hard rules
- Licences/registrations required for regulated trades (electricians, gas, etc.) — show
  numbers if the client wants and they're valid.
- Guarantees: only real, written terms.
- Service-area business: hide address on Google if no premises open to customers.

## Schema
`Plumber`, `Electrician`, `HVACBusiness`, `RoofingContractor`, `GeneralContractor`,
`HousePainter`, `Locksmith`, with `areaServed` list.

## CTAs
Call (primary), WhatsApp with photos of the problem, quote request form (for larger jobs).

## Photos
Real jobs before/after, the van with branding, the team, tools — never stock photos of
smiling models in helmets.

## FAQ ideas
Do you do emergencies? Which areas? Free quotes? How soon can you come? Payment methods?
Guarantee?

## Discovery questions
Which jobs are most profitable/which to avoid? Area radius? Emergency availability? Licences?
Who answers the phone during jobs (→ WhatsApp/form)? Photos of past jobs?

## Pitfalls
Listing services they don't want; generic stock; no area info; forms nobody checks.

## Upsells
Per-service pages for SEO in their area (only if real demand); review collection QR on
invoices; van/vehicle graphics with QR (partner printer).
