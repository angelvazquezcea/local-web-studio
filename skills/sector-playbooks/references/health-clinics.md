# Health: dentists, physiotherapists, clinics, psychologists, vets

## Goals
Appointments, trust in professionals, clear information on treatments and insurance,
fewer administrative calls.

## Audiences
Patients (anxious, older, parents), referrers, insurance patients; for vets, pet owners.

## Must-have sections
Hero + Book/Call · treatments/services (plain explanations) · professionals with
qualifications and **registration numbers** · insurance/mutual companies accepted · first visit
— what to expect · prices (if allowed and wanted) · facilities & accessibility · hours,
emergencies · location/parking · contact.

## Hard rules
- **Health advertising is regulated**: no guarantees of results, no misleading before/after,
  no testimonials where prohibited; professional association codes apply (e.g. Spain: health
  centre authorisation number shown; professional college rules).
- Health data is special-category data: **no health details in contact forms** unless the
  form is properly secured and the privacy notice covers it; prefer "call us" for clinical
  questions.
- Vets: similar registration rules; animal medicines advertising restrictions.

## Schema
`Dentist`, `Physiotherapy`, `MedicalClinic`, `Optician`, `VeterinaryCare`, `Psychologist`
(via `MedicalBusiness`), with `medicalSpecialty` where fitting.

## CTAs
Book (their appointment system), Call, emergency number (if any).

## Photos
Professionals (friendly, real), facilities clean and accessible, equipment; never graphic
clinical images.

## FAQ ideas
Do you accept my insurance? First visit cost? Parking/accessibility? Emergencies? Payment in
instalments? Children?

## Discovery questions
Authorisation/registration numbers? Insurance list? Booking software? Which treatments to
promote? Professional association advertising rules they follow? Staff to feature?

## Pitfalls
Medical claims; stock photos of doctors; forms collecting symptoms; outdated insurance lists.

## Upsells
Per-treatment information pages; accessibility audit; multilingual for expat/tourist areas.
