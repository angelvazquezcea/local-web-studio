# Rural tourism, rural houses, small hotels, campsites

## Goals
**Direct bookings** (no platform commission), fewer repetitive questions, more off-season and
weekday stays, groups/events, return guests. The site competes with how good they look on
Booking/Airbnb.

## Audiences
Couples, families, groups of friends, walkers/cyclists/pilgrims, pet owners, foreign tourists,
companies (retreats), event organisers.

## Must-have sections
Hero with the house in its landscape + Book/Check availability · the house (capacity,
rooms, beds, bathrooms — exact) · rooms/units each with photos · amenities (wifi, parking,
heating, fireplace, kitchen, garden, pool, pets, accessibility) · prices/seasons (or "ask") ·
how to book and policies (check-in/out, deposit, cancellation) · surroundings (walks, beaches,
villages, food, Camino routes — with distances) · how to get here · reviews · FAQ · contact.

## Hard rules
- **Tourism registry number** must usually be shown on the site and in ads (e.g. Spain: regional
  registry codes; Galicia, Asturias, etc. have their own formats). Ask for it.
- Prices including taxes; cancellation and deposit terms clear (consumer law).
- If they take bookings/payments directly, legal terms and withdrawal-right rules for
  accommodation services apply.
- Don't copy texts/photos from their Booking/Airbnb listings if those were written by the
  platform or a former provider without rights; rewrite.

## Schema
`BedAndBreakfast`, `LodgingBusiness`, `Hotel`, `Campground`, `VacationRental` as fitting; with
`amenityFeature`, `numberOfRooms`, `petsAllowed`, `checkinTime`, `checkoutTime`, `geo`.

## CTAs
Book (direct engine or WhatsApp with dates/guests template), Call, Check on Booking/Airbnb
(secondary), Directions.

## Photos
Exterior in good light/season, every room, bathrooms, kitchen, common areas, garden/views,
details (breakfast, fireplace), surroundings, drone of the property and landscape.

## FAQ ideas
Pets? Minimum stay? Children/cots? Parking? Wifi/coverage? Heating? How far to beach/town/
supermarket? Can we rent the whole house? Events allowed? Check-in times/self check-in?

## Discovery questions
Booking channels used and commission paid? Occupancy by season — what do they want to fill?
Direct booking tool or channel manager? Registry number? Languages of guests? Best reviews?
Rules (noise, pets, events)? Nearby partners (restaurants, activities) to recommend?

## Pitfalls
Booking button that leads nowhere; outdated prices; missing registry number; heavy galleries;
availability calendars built by hand (use their channel manager's widget or link).

## Upsells
Direct booking engine set-up via their provider; multilingual (EN/FR/DE/PT typical); drone
video; seasonal offers updates in the care plan; guest welcome guide page (QR in the house).
