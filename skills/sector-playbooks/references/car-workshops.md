# Car workshops, tyres, bodyshops

## Goals
Appointments for service/MOT-ITV prep, repairs, tyres; trust on prices and honesty; insurance
work for bodyshops.

## Audiences
Local drivers, fleets/companies, insurance clients, rural customers with vans and 4x4s.

## Must-have sections
Hero + Call/Book · services (maintenance, diagnostics, tyres, brakes, AC, bodywork, ITV/MOT
pre-check) · brands/vehicles they work on · courtesy car/pick-up (if offered) · insurance
companies they work with (if authorised) · hours · team & equipment · reviews · contact.

## Hard rules
- Brand logos only if authorised; factual "we service X brand" text is fine.
- Prices "from" must be honest and include VAT where required.
- Workshop registration numbers where locally required.

## Schema
`AutoRepair`, `AutoBodyShop`, `TireShop`, with `openingHoursSpecification`, `areaServed`.

## CTAs
Call, WhatsApp (photo of warning light/damage), book appointment.

## Photos
Clean workshop, diagnostic equipment, team, real cars being worked on (blur plates), waiting area.

## FAQ ideas
Do I need an appointment? How long does a service take? Do you work with my insurance?
Courtesy car? Warranty on repairs?

## Discovery questions
Most profitable services? Capacity per day? Booking method? Insurance agreements? Specialisms
(4x4, agricultural vehicles, EV/hybrid)?

## Pitfalls
Licence plates visible in photos; unrealistic "from" prices; no hours.

## Upsells
Online appointment link; seasonal campaigns (tyres, AC) updates; review QR at reception.
