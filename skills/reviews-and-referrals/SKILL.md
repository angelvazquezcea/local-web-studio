# Reviews and referrals

## For the studio

**When to ask:** right after the client sees the live site and says something nice — not
weeks later. Ask for three things, one at a time if needed:
1. **Google review** of the studio's Business Profile (send the direct review link).
2. **Portfolio permission** — show the site, mention the business name, use a screenshot.
   Record yes/no in `client.md`. Separate permission for photos/drone footage.
3. **Referrals** — "who else do you know who'd need this?" Ask for 1–3 names and permission
   to mention them.

Message template (adapt, keep warm and short):
> Thanks, <name>! Glad you like it. If you have a minute, a review on Google helps me a lot:
> <link>. And if you know anyone else with a business who needs a website, I'd be grateful
> if you passed on my number.

**Testimonials:** turn their words into a 1–2 sentence quote and **ask them to approve the
exact text** before publishing. Use first name + business.

## For the client's business

- Generate the client's Google review link (from their Business Profile) and suggest how to
  share it: printed QR at the counter/reception, end-of-stay message, invoice footer.
- Coach replies: thank by name, mention something specific, keep it short; for negative
  reviews, calm, factual, offer to talk privately. Draft replies on request.

## Rules (non-negotiable)

- Never write fake reviews, never post reviews on behalf of anyone, never suggest family/staff
  reviews.
- Don't offer discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews (against Google policy and consumer
  law in many countries). Asking everyone, without filtering happy customers only, is the safe
  practice.
- Real testimonials only, with approval.

## Referral partners

Track referrers in `studio.md` and thank each referral (message or agreed reward from
`pricing-packages`). See `partnerships` for structured partner deals.
