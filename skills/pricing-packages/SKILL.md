# Pricing and packages

Local clients buy certainty: one clear price, what's included, and who to call. Few options,
plainly worded, beat a menu of features.

## 1. Start from the floor, not from competitors

`floor = (hours per project × target hourly rate) + direct costs (domain year 1, stock
photos, fonts, tools share)`

Typical hours for a one-page or small multi-page local site done well: call 1.5 h, design &
build 10–20 h, copy 2–4 h, revisions 2–5 h, delivery + Google profile 2–3 h, admin 1–2 h.
Show the user this calculation with their numbers. If their price is below the floor, say
so plainly and suggest scope or price changes; it is their decision.

## 2. Package structure (default proposal)

- **Core package** — one price. Design for the business, mobile-first build, contact
  buttons (call/WhatsApp/map), basic local SEO, legal pages, Google Business Profile set-up,
  publication on the client's own hosting account, domain year 1, N revision rounds,
  first year of care.
- **Add-ons** (only the 3–5 most requested): extra language, extra pages/sections, photo or
  drone session (partner), copywriting from scratch, booking integration, simple shop/catalog,
  logo refresh, printed material (cards, signage QR).
- **Care plan from year 2** — yearly fee: domain renewal, hosting oversight, backups,
  N small changes, yearly report, priority support. State it from the first conversation.
- **Out of scope** — explicitly: e-commerce at scale, ads management, social media posting,
  monthly content, unless offered.

Three tiers are optional; for most local clients one package + add-ons is clearer.

## 3. Words matter

- Say **"included"**, not "free" (free suggests low value or a catch).
- Say **"care"/"maintenance"**, not "subscription" (subscription sounds like a trap).
- Give one total, not an hourly breakdown. Taxes: say if VAT is included or added.
- Anchor with value: "one booking/week pays it back in X weeks" — only with realistic numbers
  the client confirms.

## 4. Payment terms

Deposit 30–50% to start (books the slot), rest on delivery before transfer of the final
site, or in agreed instalments. Care plan billed yearly in advance on the anniversary.
Late payment: site stays live (client owns it) but changes pause.

## 5. Special deals without devaluing

- **First clients / portfolio deal:** a clear, time-limited reduced price *in exchange for*
  a written review, permission to show the site, and 2–3 referrals. Always state the normal
  price next to it so the anchor survives.
- **Referral reward:** a discount on the referrer's next care year, or a fixed thank-you.
- **Bundles with partners** (e.g. drone + web): one combined price, each party invoices its
  part or one invoices and pays the other — agree in writing.
- Never cut price for the same scope after an objection; reduce scope instead.

## 6. Recurring income view

When the user sets goals (e.g. monthly income target), show the arithmetic: new sites per
month × price + active care plans × yearly fee / 12. Point out that care plans compound and
retention depends on making value visible during year 1 (see `care-plans`).

## Output

A short pricing table, the reasoning in 3–5 bullets, and any risks. Write it to `studio.md`
under "Offer" only if the user approves.
