# Service agreement

Small clients rarely want a long contract, but a one-to-two page agreement prevents the
classic disputes: endless changes, unpaid balance, who owns the domain, "you used my photos".

**Always say:** this is a practical template, not legal advice; for anything unusual, the user
should have it reviewed by a local professional. Adapt to the jurisdiction in `studio.md`.

## Clauses to include (plain language)

1. **Parties** — studio and client legal names, tax IDs, addresses.
2. **Scope** — reference the accepted proposal (date). List deliverables. Anything else is a
   change request quoted separately.
3. **Client duties** — provide texts/photos/data by agreed dates; review within N days; the
   client is responsible for the truth of the information and for having rights to the
   material they provide.
4. **Revisions** — N rounds included; a round = one consolidated list of changes. Extra
   rounds or new sections are quoted.
5. **Timeline** — the demo date depends on receiving materials; delays on the client side move
   dates. Projects paused > 60 days without response may be closed and invoiced for work done.
6. **Payment** — total, VAT, deposit (non-refundable once work starts), balance due on
   delivery, method, late payment handling.
7. **Ownership** —
   - Domain, hosting account and Google Business Profile are registered **in the client's
     name**; the studio has delegated access to manage them.
   - On full payment, the client owns the final site content and design files delivered.
     The studio keeps rights to its generic code, tools and know-how.
   - Third-party licences (stock photos, fonts) follow their own terms.
8. **Portfolio** — the studio may show the site in its portfolio **only if the client ticks
   yes**. Separate tick for showing drone/photo material.
9. **Care plan** — what it includes (domain renewal, hosting oversight, N small changes,
   backups, yearly report), price from year 2, billed yearly, cancellation with 30 days'
   notice before renewal; on cancellation the studio hands over all access.
10. **Liability** — the studio is not liable for third-party outages (hosting, registrar,
    Google), for client-provided content, or for indirect losses; total liability capped at
    fees paid in the last 12 months.
11. **Data protection** — if the site collects form data, who is the controller (the client)
    and the studio's role as processor during maintenance (EU: a short data processing clause).
12. **Termination** — either party with written notice; work done is paid; access handed over.
13. **Law & signatures** — jurisdiction, date, signatures (e-signature is fine).

## Output

A Markdown agreement in `docs/agreement-<date>.md` filled from `client.md` + proposal, with
remaining blanks marked `[TO FILL]`. Offer a one-paragraph WhatsApp summary of the key terms.
