# Lead prospecting

Local web work is won by being useful to a specific kind of business in a specific area,
not by mass messaging. Quality over quantity: 20 well-researched leads beat 500 scraped ones.

## 1. Define the hunting ground

Read `studio.md` for sectors and area. If missing, ask: which sectors, which towns/radius,
and whether the user has personal connections there (warm leads convert far better).

## 2. Sources (in order of quality)

1. **Warm network** — family, friends, former employers, neighbours, clients' suppliers.
   Ask the user to list 10 names before doing any research. This is usually the best list.
2. **Partners** — photographers, drone operators, gestorías/accountants, printers,
   agricultural cooperatives, tourism associations, local business associations.
3. **Google Maps** — search "<sector> in <town>"; note businesses with no website link,
   few photos, old or unanswered reviews, wrong hours.
4. **Sector directories** — cooperatives, denomination-of-origin registries, rural tourism
   portals, chambers of commerce, trade guilds, local fairs and markets exhibitors.
5. **Social media** — businesses that only have a Facebook/Instagram page.

Use web search/fetch tools when available. Never scrape personal data in bulk. Store only
business contact data that the business itself publishes.

## 3. Presence audit per lead (10 minutes each)

| Check | What to note |
|---|---|
| Website | none / broken / not mobile / slow / outdated (years in footer, Flash, http) / OK |
| Google Business Profile | missing / unclaimed / incomplete (hours, photos, category) / OK |
| Reviews | count, average, last date, owner replies? |
| Phone & WhatsApp | visible? clickable? |
| Social | active? last post date |
| Competitors nearby | do they have good sites? (useful argument) |
| Hook | one concrete thing you could improve for them in a sentence |

## 4. Score and prioritise

Score 1–3 on each: **need** (how bad their presence is), **value** (can they pay, do they
gain from customers finding them online), **access** (warm contact, partner, cold).
Contact the highest total first. Businesses that sell to the public, take bookings or
compete locally (tourism, restaurants, trades) feel the value fastest. Businesses that only
sell to one buyer (e.g. an integrated farm) want image/recruitment/trust, not sales — pitch
accordingly.

## 5. Output

Write `leads.md` (or the location in `studio.md`) as a table:

`| Business | Sector | Town | Contact (public) | Presence issues | Hook | Score | Status | Next step |`

Status: `new` / `contacted` / `replied` / `call booked` / `not now` / `no`.
Then suggest the first 3–5 to contact and hand off to `outreach-messages`.

## Ethics and law

- In the EU (GDPR/ePrivacy) cold email to companies is restricted; personal WhatsApp/SMS
  marketing without consent is not allowed. Prefer in-person visits, phone calls to a
  published business line, introductions, and replies to public enquiries. When unsure,
  tell the user and suggest the safer channel.
- Be honest in audits: never exaggerate problems or invent "your site is losing X customers".
