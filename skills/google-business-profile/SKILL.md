# Google Business Profile

It's what appears on Google Maps and in the local pack. For many local businesses it brings
more calls than the website itself. It is part of a complete delivery.

## Ownership

- The **client** is the primary owner (their Google account). The studio is **manager**.
  If the studio created it under its own account, transfer primary ownership to the client.
- Never ask for the client's Google password. Ask them to add the studio as manager, or sit
  with them (call/visit) while they do it.

## Create or claim

1. Search the business on Google Maps first: it may exist (created by users). Claim it
   instead of creating a duplicate.
2. Business name: the real-world name only — no keywords or towns added (policy violation,
   risk of suspension).
3. **Category**: the most specific primary category; up to a few secondary ones. This is the
   strongest ranking factor you control.
4. Address vs **service area**: show the address only if customers come to the place. Trades
   that go to customers and places that don't accept visits (e.g. farms with biosecurity rules)
   should hide the address and set a service area — and the site must say the same.
5. Pin: check the pin precisely for rural places; fix it if Maps puts it on the wrong road.
6. Phone, website (with UTM parameters if analytics will separate traffic:
   `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=gbp`), hours, special hours.
7. **Verification**: Google decides the method (video, phone/SMS, email, postcard). Prepare the
   client: a video may need showing signage, the place and proof of business. It can take days.

## Optimise

- Description (≤ 750 chars): what, where, for whom, differentiators; no URLs or promos.
- Services/products with short descriptions (and prices if public).
- Attributes (accessibility, payment, amenities).
- Photos: logo, cover, exterior (helps people find it), interior, products, team, at least 10;
  then add new ones every month or two (care plan touchpoint).
- Posts for news, seasons, events (optional; low effort, signals activity).
- Q&A seeded with real frequent questions if the feature is available.
- Booking/menu links where relevant.

## Reviews

Get the short review link from the profile; give it to the client with a QR
(`reviews-and-referrals`). Coach replies; draft replies on request.

## Common problems

- **Duplicate listings** → request removal/merge via the profile's support options.
- **Suspension** → usually keyword-stuffed names, virtual offices, wrong address type. Fix
  the cause, then request reinstatement with proof (licences, utility bills, photos of signage).
- **Wrong info suggested by users** → owners must review "Google updates" regularly.

## Hand over

Record in `client.md`: profile URL, review link, owner/manager emails (not passwords),
verification status and date. Mention insights (calls, direction requests, website clicks)
will be included in the yearly report.
