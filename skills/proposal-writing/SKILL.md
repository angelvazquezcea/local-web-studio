# Proposal writing

A local proposal is one or two pages the owner can read on a phone and show to a partner.
It proves you listened. It is not a technical spec.

## Inputs

`client.md` (brief), `studio.md` (offer, prices, terms), sector playbook. If price or scope is
unclear, ask before writing. Never invent deliverables the studio has not offered.

## Structure

1. **Title** — "Website for <Business>" + date + validity (e.g. 30 days).
2. **What you told me** — 3–5 bullets in the client's words: their situation and goals
   ("people from Madrid ask how to get here", "we want more weekday bookings").
3. **What the website will do** — outcomes, not features: easy to call/WhatsApp from the
   phone, appear on Google and Maps, show <their differentiator>, answer frequent questions,
   in <languages>.
4. **What's included** — bullet list from the core package, tailored. Name the sections
   proposed (from `site-planning`). Include Google Business Profile, domain, hosting set-up
   in their name, legal pages, N revision rounds, first-year care.
5. **Not included / optional** — add-ons with prices; anything explicitly excluded.
6. **How we'll work** — steps with dates: materials → demo in ~N days → revision rounds →
   publication → handover. What the client must provide and by when.
7. **Investment** — one total. VAT note. Payment terms (deposit, balance). Care plan from
   year 2 with price and what it covers.
8. **Next step** — one action: "reply OK and I'll send the deposit details" / "sign below".
9. **Example** — link to one relevant site from the portfolio.

## Style

- Their language, plain words, short sentences. No jargon.
- Specific to them; if the proposal could be sent to another business unchanged, rewrite.
- No discounts hidden in small print; if there is a special deal, state normal price, deal
  price and what they give in return.

## Formats

- Default: Markdown in `docs/proposal-<date>.md`, plus a WhatsApp-sized summary (≤ 6 lines).
- If the user wants a PDF/Word file or a shareable page, use the available document or
  artifact tools; keep the same content.

Update `client.md` status to `proposal-sent` with the date and the proposed price.
