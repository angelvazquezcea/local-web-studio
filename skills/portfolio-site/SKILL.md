# The studio's own site

The studio site is a sales tool for people who will look at it on a phone after hearing about
you from a neighbour. It must answer in 10 seconds: what you do, for whom, how much, proof,
how to contact.

## Structure (one page is enough)

1. **Hero** — one sentence offer for the audience ("Websites for farms, rural houses and local
   businesses in <region>") + call and WhatsApp buttons.
2. **Real examples** — 2–6 client sites with screenshot (phone frame), business name, one-line
   result or quote, link. Only with portfolio permission. Until then, one honest example
   (a demo labelled as such, or a friend/family business).
3. **What's included** — the core package in plain bullets with the word "included".
4. **Price** — "From X" or a fixed price + what care costs from year 2. Transparent prices filter
   bad leads and build trust with local clients.
5. **How it works** — 4 steps: call → demo in ~N days → changes → published + Google profile.
6. **Reviews** — real Google reviews (embed as text with link, not a heavy widget).
7. **About** — photo, name, where you are, why you do this. Local identity matters.
8. **Contact** — call, WhatsApp, email, area served. Optional simple form.
9. **Partners/packs** — e.g. drone + web, if any.

## Rules

- Follow the full technical baseline (`static-site-build`, `contact-actions`, `local-seo` with
  `ProfessionalService` schema and the area served).
- Its design must not look like any client's site; it can be the most restrained of all.
- Keep it updated: every delivered client with permission → add to examples (ask the user).
- Google Business Profile for the studio too; reviews there feed the site.
